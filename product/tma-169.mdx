## Summary

TMA-169: **Mua xe** tab catalog. Public list\+filters. `fuel_type=gas|electric` is required on list.

## HTTP

`GET /api/v1/marketplace/listings/` query: brand, bike\_type, displacement buckets, power buckets (EV), location, price, odo, year, sort, page. Guide: `docs/api/MARKETPLACE_CATALOG_API.md`.

## Buy money path

Checkout is **dealer** VietQR \+ paid-hook, not a mobile-only payment service. App purchase-order URLs are **not in urls.py** yet.

## Do not

- Do not ship a buy button that hits missing `/marketplace/purchase-orders/` and call it done.
