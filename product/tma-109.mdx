## Summary

TMA-109: marketplace **bike detail** (phase 1.5) after TMA-169 catalog. Public GET detail \+ media \+ condition \+ variants (EV colors) \+ related. Git: `docs/api/MARKETPLACE_BIKE_DETAIL_API.md`.

## HTTP

`GET /api/v1/marketplace/listings/{bike_id}/` and nested `/media/`, `/condition-analysis/`, `/variants/`, `/related/`.

## Invariants

Public AllowAny. Sold/hidden listings must not appear as buyable. EV variants optional rows in `bikes_marketplace_variant`.

## Do not

- Do not require Cognito to browse detail.
- Do not confuse dealer Hub bike detail (`/api/v1/bikes/{id}/`) with this public marketplace detail.
