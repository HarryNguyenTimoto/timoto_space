## Checkout (mobile buy)

| Method | Path | Consumer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| POST | `/payments/checkouts/` | Mobile S2S / dealer |
| GET | `/payments/checkouts/{id}/` | Poll |
| POST | `/payments/checkouts/{id}/qr/` | VietQR |
| POST | `/payments/dealer-order/create/` | Dealer FE (actual FE path) |

Paid-hook: dealer → mobile `POST /api/v1/internal/payments/checkout-paid/` HMAC. Full text: dealer `docs/api/SHARED_CHECKOUT_CONTRACT.md`.

## FE drift

Dealer v2 `APIs.ts` uses `/payments/dealer-order/create/` not always CONTRACT `POST /orders/{id}/pay/`. Notifications in CONTRACT may not be in `APIs.ts` (OneSignal separate).
